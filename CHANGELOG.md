# Changelog

## 0.10.0 — World layout, prizes, ads & store
- New world layout: **5 / 5 / 6 levels**, then 8 per world. One new idea per level in the early worlds. World 1 shows a 2-bounce aim guide.
- The first **Dark World** is now **World 4** (then 8, 12, then every 5th world). It brings no new hazards: darkness is the only new idea.
- Old saves migrate automatically: you keep your world and its stars.
- **World prizes**: the first clear of a world gives a themed power-up. Dark Worlds give a Searchlight for the next Dark World, and the first one also unlocks the **Eclipse** trail.
- **Rewarded ads** (opt-in, five placements with caps), behind an adapter for the Android app. `?ads=test` previews them.
- **Store**: free daily power-up, a ball-trail picker (Classic / Eclipse / Aurora), and packs including the Ad-free Pass. `?iap=test` previews purchases.
- Readability fixes and a "Hard" label driven by the level data.

## 0.9.x — Beta
- PWA (install + offline), GA4 analytics, Game Over "Start World Over", lit sun in Dark Worlds, higher slingshot with more power, splash → map transition, visual-only tutorials, dynamic revive price, daily bonus, star rules, aim assist.
